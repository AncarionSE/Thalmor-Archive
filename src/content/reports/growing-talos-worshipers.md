## Introduction

During my off duty hours, I have been checking in hotspots for talos worshipers, from taverns to temples a few have came to mind and information has also been presented towards me. Currently riften and whiterun have the largest presences of them, from rumours and conversations of civilians the number ranges between 5–10 people who are actively worshiping the man god.

They have been using codewords and also cyphers or coded messages, some of the ones I have discovered were:

- **HLNPT** — Unknown
- **SSBF** — Skyrim Shall Be Free
- **LOIR** — Lack of imperials riften
- **MUSDTE** — Martyred Ulfric Stormcloak, death to elfs

It seems they are using these to communicate or spread messages between notice boards.

Of course you do have those who run around wearing the white miner clothes, mostly of the beast races, who shout about talos — this report will not include them.

## Riften: Talos Worshipers

The rifts population seems to be all over the place, with the recent attacks from supernatural a lot of their guardsmen have resigned their post, largely because they need to actually do their job for once instead of standing around idle.

This has lead to several issues in the enforcement of local laws and the W-G-C.

Shor stone is normally the primary location for these people to remain, between 10am to 11am skyrim time they are seen gathering in groups of 3–4. Upon seeing an outsider they do not recognize they do scatter in all four directions. Their exact location of where they proceed to is unknown.

It seems these groups are becoming more and more trained in preventing capture or spilling that of information.

## Stormcloak Surplus

Nords wearing the stormcloak surplus armor from the human's civil war are to be looked with suspicion.

Windhelm seems to have a decent sized population of around 4–15 people who commonly wear it around the place, always talking in whispers with similar people wearing it, asking those not part of their friend group to leave them alone.

Non nords have also been seen wearing these armors, their hostility to altmer is noted. Rather than supporting the stormcloaks, I feel these people represent opposition to the thalmor rather than the ideals of a 'free skyrim'.

They are yet to make a large scale movement against anyone or anything outside of reports normally suggested to be bandits.

The armor is largely that of the heavy versions, clearly made for war rather than that of protection. The capes are largely rugged blue capes or black/white fur capes, clearly being used as somekind of identification.

## Closing Points / Unconfirmed

The hold of whiterun is not enforcing the W-G-C, this is of course common knowledge at this point. Talos worshipers are not being arrested for their heretical views, even that of the legion are looking the other way.

Rumours about pilgrimages to high hrothgar are being conducted by nords.

The shrine of talos within that of markarth has been seeing some visitors, most of them were not nords however; the guardsmen of that hold do not seem to stop people from entering it.

Whitecloaked bandits have been attacking the legion's forts, attempting to kill that of imperials.
