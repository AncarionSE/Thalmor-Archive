## Report

Kitra Freewind, the leader of the Northstar Rangers, was arrested in Whiterun on the implication that she was bloodkin to Vashu Agra. She was placed under sentence of execution due to this alleged association.

In response, Ghorlak Uzgolg rallied his clan and marched on Whiterun as a show of force. He issued a twelve-hour ultimatum, stating that if Kitra was not released and the execution proceeded, Whiterun would face siege.

The situation was presented to Lord Regent Sees-His-Scales.

A meeting later took place between the Vashu Agra host and Whiterun. Following this meeting, the bounty against Vashu Agra as a clan was dropped. However, their banishment from Whiterun lands remains in effect.

## Reference Context

The banishment of Vashu Agra from Whiterun lands reportedly stems from a prior incident in which a Whiterun Khajiit challenged Ghorlak Uzgolg to a fist fight. This escalated into the Whiterun Court marching on Vashu Agra, where the chieftain defended his hold with four other Orcs. Seven members of the Whiterun entourage were killed, and the remaining party fled.

**Note:** The Windhelm Guard was assisting Whiterun with protection against the Orcs during this matter.
