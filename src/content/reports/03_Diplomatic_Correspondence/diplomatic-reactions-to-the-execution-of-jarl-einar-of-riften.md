# Intelligence Report

**Reporting Officer:** Chief Administrator & Quartermaster Ancarion Saelthar
**Subject:** Diplomatic Reactions to the Execution of Jarl Einar of Riften

A representative of the **Court of Windhelm** arrived at the Embassy seeking clarification regarding the execution of **Jarl Einar of Riften**. The representative questioned the reasoning behind the Dominion's actions.

I informed the representative that the Aldmeri Dominion acts solely in accordance with the provisions of the **White-Gold Concordat**, and that any official clarification or diplomatic discussion would be handled by members of the Embassy's **Diplomatic Branch** should the Court of Windhelm require further explanation.

Following this exchange, I accompanied several Dominion soldiers and **Master Smith Ma'nita Stormbreaker** on a patrol to **Dawnstar**, where we met with **Lady Regent Bryn**.

Lady Regent Bryn expressed considerable anger regarding the execution of Jarl Einar, stating that she had known him personally and describing the decision as **"abhorrent,"** citing what she perceived as only a brief period of trial before the execution was carried out.

<!--page-->

I explained that the Dominion's actions were conducted in accordance with the terms of the **White-Gold Concordat**, and assured her that our Diplomatic Branch would make every effort to address concerns raised by the various Holds and provide clarification where appropriate.

Although the Lady Regent remained visibly distressed, she eventually apologized for her harsh reception, explaining that Dawnstar is currently facing significant internal difficulties, most notably the disappearance of the **Guardian of the Pale**.

I expressed my sympathy regarding the situation and offered Dominion assistance should the Hold require additional security support or other aid. I further informed the Lady Regent that the Guardian of the Pale had been a personal acquaintance and that we had previously exchanged security-related intelligence.

The meeting concluded on cordial terms. Lady Regent Bryn wished me well and expressed her hope that our next meeting would take place under more favorable circumstances.

<!--page-->

### Assessment

The execution of Jarl Einar has generated considerable political concern beyond Riften, with both **Windhelm** and **Dawnstar** seeking clarification or expressing dissatisfaction. While the Dominion's legal position under the White-Gold Concordat was communicated consistently, diplomatic engagement with the Holds should continue in order to reduce tensions and preserve existing relationships.

The disappearance of the Guardian of the Pale may warrant further observation, as it represents a potential security concern within Dawnstar and could contribute to the Hold's current instability.
