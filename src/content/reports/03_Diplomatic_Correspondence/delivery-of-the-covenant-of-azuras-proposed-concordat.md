**To:** High Justiciar Lakkon Lourinien
**From:** Chief Administrator Ancarion Saelthar
**Subject:** Proposed Concordat

High Justiciar,

J'Ocelot, leader of the **Covenant of Azura**, arrived at the Embassy in response to your request for a meeting regarding the proposed Concordat between the Aldmeri Dominion and the Covenant.

As you were unavailable upon his arrival, he entrusted me with the draft Concordat for delivery. The document has been received into Embassy custody without incident and has been placed upon your desk for your review.

<!--page-->

No statements, commitments, or assurances were made on behalf of the Dominion. I informed J'Ocelot only that the proposal would be presented directly to you for consideration.

Respectfully,

**Chief Administrator**
**Ancarion Saelthar**
Administrative Wing
Thalmor Embassy
