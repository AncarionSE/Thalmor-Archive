## Field Report Notes
**Compiled by:** Ancarion Saelthar
**Purpose:** Consolidated activity and intelligence record

### 1. Agra Near Kynesgrove
An individual or group identified as **Agra** was noted above **Kynesgrove**. Associated names include **Chieftain Yoberk** and **Warleader Bog the Fearless**. Further verification is required regarding their allegiance, purpose, and current activity.

### 2. Windhelm Court Report
At the **Court of Windhelm**, **Jacenth** reported an individual named **Bjorn**, believed to be affiliated with the **Sons of Skyrim**. The matter should be treated as politically sensitive due to the group's rebel leanings.

### 3. Talks-at-Night
**Talks-at-Night**, an Argonian, was observed mainly near the **Solitude blacksmith**. There are suspicions of possible **Daedric worship**, though his behavior appeared harmless at the time of observation. Continued discreet monitoring is advised.

### 4. Disruptors Guild Meeting Request
**Casseth Verethi**, head of the **Disruptors Guild**, requested an official meeting with **First Emissary Malen** regarding matters of magic. This request may warrant formal review before approval.

### 5. Report from Falon Kaeius
**Falon Kaeius** reported **Falgrim Hammersmite** near **Shor's Bridge**, close to Riften. Falgrim was accused of open **Talos worship** and inciting violence against the Thalmor.

### 6. Legion Officer Report
A Legion officer named **Aurelius** expressed interest in reporting a possible **Talos worshipper**. Follow-up is recommended to obtain names, location, and supporting details.

### 9. Incident at Mor Khazgur
The Orcs of **Mor Khazgur** became severely offended after **Malacath** was insulted. This led to a fight with a **Vigilant of Stendarr**. One Orc, identified as **Gork the Red**, declared that he was not afraid of elves and showed open hostility. The situation may indicate strong religious sensitivity among the Orcs and possible resistance to Thalmor authority.

### 10. Battle of the Bards
An event known as the **Battle of the Bards** is scheduled in **Riften** on **Saturday at 8 PM**. Attendance or surveillance may provide useful intelligence, especially if political songs or anti-Thalmor sentiment are present.

### 11. Bomser and Magical Tomes
**Bomser**, reportedly connected to the **Disruptors Guild**, was seen buying spell tomes. His intentions remain unknown. Continued observation is recommended.

### 12. Possible Vampire: Merllidoon Sillonal
A possible vampire named **Merllidoon Sillonal** was encountered at the **Winking Skeever Inn** in Solitude. He bore unusual facial markings and a strange eye, possibly consistent with vampiric traits. He claimed to be a wanderer from **Black Marsh**. After the conversation, he reportedly vanished outside the Solitude gates. He has since joined the **Morthal Militia** and remains in pursuit of magical tomes. This subject should be considered suspicious and worth tracking.

### 13. Bomadil Arrest Incident
**Bomadil** resisted arrest, fled, and ran off a cliff. The conduct was irregular and disruptive. Further action depends on whether the individual survived and whether witnesses can confirm the full sequence of events.

## Summary
Several matters require follow-up: suspected Talos worship, possible Daedric activity, magical tome acquisition, and the suspicious behavior of Merllidoon Sillonal. The strongest immediate concerns are **Bjorn of the Sons of Skyrim**, **Falgrim Hammersmite**, and **Merllidoon Sillonal**.
