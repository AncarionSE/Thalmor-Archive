# Intelligence Correlation Report

**Subject:** Lord Commander Leo Ironborn, Whiterun Guard

<!--page-->

## Executive Summary

Multiple independent intelligence reports suggest that **Lord Commander Leo Ironborn** has adopted policies and made public statements that conflict with the enforcement of the White-Gold Concordat. While no single report conclusively links him to the Blades or organized Talos worship, the cumulative evidence indicates ideological opposition to Dominion authority and possible sympathy toward anti-Dominion elements.

<!--page-->

## 1. Refusal to Enforce the White-Gold Concordat

**Report Source:** Officer Falcril Caemorin. **Corroborated by:** Recruit Valerion, Guard Darker-Than-Stars, direct conversation with Leo.

Commander Leo confirmed that Whiterun Guards would identify Talos worshippers but **not apprehend them** — a clear departure from previous cooperation between Whiterun and Dominion authorities.

<!--page-->

## 2. Publicly Hostile Statements

Independent reports from **Sinmo Aldor** and **Althenar Nuramor** document Commander Leo publicly stating, before Whiterun Guards, Legionnaires, and civilians, that the Thalmor would never receive the keys to his dungeon, that the Dominion would have to make arrests independently, that the Thalmor were "prancing around the Holds," and that he expected they would eventually "lose their heads." One guard reportedly responded, "Yeah, fuck 'em."

**Assessment:** This demonstrates not merely disagreement but active encouragement of anti-Dominion sentiment among Whiterun's security forces.

<!--page-->

## 3. Policy Reversal

Earlier intelligence (Arendor Raelendis) recorded Whiterun Guards detaining Talos worshippers, assisting Dominion interrogations, and applauding executions carried out under the White-Gold Concordat. Current intelligence shows Commander Leo has ordered no arrests, observation only — a significant shift coinciding with his command.

<!--page-->

## 4. Previous Diplomatic Correlation

Following Jarl Einar's execution, Whiterun questioned Dominion actions and diplomatic tensions increased, requiring negotiation before restrictions on Dominion access were lifted. Leo's current rhetoric aligns with this broader political unease.

<!--page-->

## 5. Possible Blade Correlation

Previous Embassy intelligence reported Akaviri swords in Whiterun, renewed interest in Karthspire, and the Companions forging and distributing Akaviri katanas — openly described as "Blade's swords" — to members of the Jarl's court. Commander Leo has not been observed carrying such a weapon but commands personnel operating within this environment.

<!--page-->

## 6. Existing Blade Investigations

Separate reports identify Papa Gribb, Meridian worshippers, Fort Greymoor, and possible Blade sympathizers. Commander Leo now occupies a command position in a region where several independent investigations point toward renewed Blade activity.

<!--page-->

## 7. Possible Motive

Commander Leo appears motivated by opposition to Dominion judicial authority, a desire to maintain Whiterun's legal independence, and reluctance to allow Dominion enforcement within his jurisdiction. Whether this stems from political disagreement, Nord nationalism, sympathy for Talos worshippers, or Blade influence remains undetermined.

<!--page-->

## Intelligence Gaps

The following remain unconfirmed: membership in the Blades, Talos worship, coordination with known anti-Dominion organizations, direct involvement in supplying Blade weaponry, and participation in anti-Dominion conspiracies.

## Recommended Actions

- Continue discreet surveillance of Commander Leo.
- Monitor changes in Whiterun Guard enforcement.
- Identify officers loyal to Leo.
- Determine whether Talos worshippers increase following the policy change.

<!--page-->

- Monitor contact with Companions and known Blade sympathizers.
- Cross-reference future reports involving Leo with intelligence from Whiterun, the Companions, and Fort Greymoor.

<!--page-->

## Overall Assessment

While there is **no direct evidence** that Commander Leo Ironborn is a member of the Blades or a Talos worshipper, the cumulative reporting establishes a consistent pattern: he has halted enforcement of White-Gold Concordat provisions concerning Talos worship within Whiterun, made openly hostile public statements toward the Dominion, and commands a guard force operating in an environment where Akaviri "Blade's swords" are being manufactured and distributed to members of the Jarl's court. His policies represent a marked departure from Whiterun's previous cooperation with Dominion authorities.

<!--page-->

**Intelligence Classification:** Priority Person of Interest (POI). Continued observation is warranted. Current reporting justifies increased scrutiny but does **not** yet justify alleging membership in the Blades or active Talos worship. Further corroborating evidence would be required before making either conclusion.
