Reporting Guard: Val Shatter-Steel

Reporting Guard Rank/Assignment: Guard, Solitude City Watch

Other Guards Present: None recorded

People Involved: Unidentified author, signing as "HLNPT" (Offender, unidentified)

Summary: A notice was discovered posted on the public board in Solitude, reading in full:

<!--page-->

"Okay Blue Bros let's try this again with smaller paper! Last time the good bits were cut off, but I know many people are upset and angry, about stuff and I'm just here to remind you that a hold is just empty walls without you! No one to give business, no one to give them money, no one to hold their silly laws over or to tell to buzz off or quiet down or what to wear! Or not to wear, you do you blue! There will be more notes, call to arms, and more things to come but for now keep in mind your leaders aren't as all powerful as they think. Their power is in the hold's people. The change starts with you, so come on and wear some blue! HLNPT 1/4"

Notice is marked "1/4," indicating this is the first of an intended series rather than a single, isolated statement.

<!--page-->

Reporting Guard's Notes: I want to flag the specific detail that concerns me most, beyond the general call to defiance: the repeated, deliberate use of "blue" as an organizing color and rallying identity. Given this Hold's history, a rebellion fought and crushed within living memory, an occupation that has since thinned considerably, and a resurgent insurgency already testing the Empire's weakened hold on this province through raids and sabotage, I do not think this color choice is incidental. Blue was the Stormcloaks' banner. A notice explicitly branding its readers as "Blue Bros" and telling them their leaders' power "is in the hold's people" reads, to me, as either a genuine ideological successor to that movement or a deliberate invocation of it to recruit sympathy from people who remember what that color used to mean.

<!--page-->

The reference to a prior version being "cut off" and the explicit promise of "more notes, call to arms, and more things to come" both indicate this is an organized, ongoing effort rather than a single disgruntled citizen venting once. Given the current political climate and the scale of what a properly organized resurgence could mean for this Hold, I do not believe this is something to treat lightly or dismiss as harmless grumbling.

Action Taken: Given the potential severity and the explicit reference to further escalation, I have forwarded this notice directly to the Thalmor Embassy as a formal warning of a possible Stormcloak-aligned resurgence taking root within Solitude itself, rather than solely in the wilds where such activity has been previously reported.

<!--page-->

Disposition: Report Forwarded to Command; Report Forwarded to Thalmor Embassy

Other: Recommend the notice board be monitored closely for the promised "1/4" series' continuation, and that any citizen observed posting similar material be detained for questioning regarding "HLNPT" and its actual scope. Recommend Legion and Thalmor coordination on this matter given the stakes involved should this prove to be more than isolated rhetoric.
