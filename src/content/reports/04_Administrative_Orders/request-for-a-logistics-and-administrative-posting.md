To the Esteemed Justiciars and Dominion Leadership,

I hope this letter finds you well.

Following my recent discussions with Justiciar Lakkon and Justiciar Elvander, I wished to formally express my thoughts regarding my current duties and future service within the Dominion.

Over the course of my service, I have found myself increasingly engaged in responsibilities beyond those of a conventional soldier. In addition to my duties as a field officer, I have maintained personnel rosters, managed equipment distribution, organized archives and dossiers, supervised the forging and acquisition of arms and armor, and served as Quartermaster for our forces. I have also worked to establish and maintain productive relationships with various holds throughout Skyrim in order to secure materials and resources essential to Dominion operations.

These efforts have proven particularly valuable during periods of material scarcity. Through negotiations and personal contacts, I have been able to secure ores and refined materials at favorable rates, helping ensure the continued operation of our forge and the equipping of our personnel. As our organization grows, I believe these logistical and administrative functions will only become more important.

While I greatly enjoy training recruits, leading patrols, and assisting in the development of our soldiers, I have found that the increasing demands of administration, procurement, record keeping, and supply management are becoming substantial responsibilities in their own right. I fear that attempting to balance all of these duties simultaneously may ultimately diminish my effectiveness in each role.

As such, I would respectfully request consideration for a position more focused on logistics, administration, procurement, and resource management within the Dominion. I believe my experience as Quartermaster, archivist, instructor, and blacksmith has provided me with a unique understanding of our operational needs and the challenges involved in sustaining them.

My goal is not to step away from service to our soldiers, but rather to better serve them by ensuring that our records remain organized, our supplies remain available, our equipment remains maintained, and our relations with local authorities continue to benefit Dominion interests.

I remain fully committed to the Dominion and stand ready to serve wherever I am most useful.

Respectfully,

**Officer Ancarion Saelthar**
**Quartermaster and Officer of the Aldmeri Dominion**
