❖ ALDMERI DOMINION — THALMOR EMBASSY, SKYRIM ❖
**Administrative Notice**
**Weekly Payroll Distribution**

By order of the Office of the Chief Administrator, all personnel who are eligible for weekly wages are hereby directed to report to the Administrative Branch to receive payment for services rendered during the pay period:

**Mondas, 20th of Sun's Height, 4E 226**
through
**Sundas, 26th of Sun's Height, 4E 226**

All payments shall be collected no later than the posted deadline.

Personnel are expected to report in an orderly manner. Any discrepancies regarding wages, attendance, or payroll records shall be brought to the attention of the Quartermaster or Chief Administrator, as those in charge of payments.

For the order and efficiency of the Dominion.

Signed,

Ancarion Saelthar
Chief Administrator
Administrative Branch
Thalmor Embassy
