# Training Report

**Reporting Officer:** Officer Ancarion Saelthar
**Subject:** Auxiliary Sparring Exercises

A supervised sparring session was conducted for both soldiers and recruits. Overall performance was satisfactory, with all participants demonstrating competence and discipline throughout the exercises.

<!--page-->

One point of improvement was identified regarding the newest Orc recruit, **Perosh**. During multiple bouts, he remained largely stationary and relied heavily on power attacks early in engagements. This rapidly exhausted his stamina, leaving him unable to maintain pressure or defend effectively during the later stages of combat. Additional training focusing on stamina management, movement, and pacing is recommended.

**You'n'me** performed exceptionally well, achieving victory in all assigned sparring matches against fellow soldiers. Her composure, consistency, and combat effectiveness were highly commendable.

<!--page-->

**Kanalar** and **Fen'ril** also distinguished themselves during training, displaying strong performance and good martial discipline throughout the exercises.

### Assessment

The exercise was a success and demonstrated the overall combat readiness of participating personnel. Continued training is recommended, particularly for newer recruits, to improve endurance, tactical awareness, and battlefield discipline.
