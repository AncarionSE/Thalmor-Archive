## Riverwood Observation

A citizen by the name of Dinglemyre was witnessed by me near the forge of Riverwood saying "by the Nine" as he was talking to the local population, as they were singing and dancing. The behavior of the population in Riverwood is quite concerning, as they seem to use alien Dwemer devices for singing that produced sounds unheard before, and their behavior is overall absurd — more like trolls.
