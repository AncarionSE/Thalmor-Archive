## Report

During procuring of smithing materials from the embassy, I was met with a hooded Argonian in black hooded robes asking for information about Niris Felstar, saying she's interested in the bounty on her head on the notice board, as she is nowhere to be found. The citizen was sent off and was not provided any information, and was instructed that the individual by the name Niris Felstar is not in any way associated with the Thalmor embassy.
