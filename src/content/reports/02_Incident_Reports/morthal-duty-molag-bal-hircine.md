## Duty Report

During guard duty in Morthal, I, alongside Saurel and Vallarion, assisted Lord Regent Sees-His-Scales with town security and local matters.

We conducted multiple patrols throughout Morthal and provided assistance to the townsfolk where needed. We also maintained guard around the Lord Regent during his activities in the hold.

During the course of duty, Lord Regent Sees-His-Scales granted myself and several soldiers access to the guard keep and dungeons by providing the necessary keys.
