# Intelligence Report

**Reporting Officer:** Officer Ancarion Saelthar
**Date:** 29th of Midyear

Information was received regarding **Elenwen**, a recently inducted soldier of the Dominion.

According to reports, Elenwen was observed at **Castle Wraithmor** interacting with individuals identified as cultists of **Mehrunes Dagon**. During the incident, he was reportedly impersonating a member of the **Dragonsguard**.

Additional concerns were raised by **Chadiminus**, the tailor of Solitude, who reported being personally harassed by Elenwen on multiple occasions. Chadiminus was the individual who brought the matter to attention.

The soldier had previously displayed erratic behavior and had repeatedly expressed an unusual fixation on obtaining a set of **Black Thalmor Armor**.

### Assessment

The allegations involving association with suspected Mehrunes Dagon cultists, impersonation of another organization, repeated harassment of a civilian, and previously observed erratic conduct raise concerns regarding the soldier's judgment and suitability for service. The matter warrants further investigation to determine the accuracy of the accusations and whether disciplinary action is required.
