Supply Corps Financial Report

Reporting Officer: Canonreeve Ancarion Saelthar

Financial Summary
Category    Amount

The Supply Corps concluded the reporting period with a net operational surplus of 14,000 Gold. Current expenditures remained below projected income, allowing the department to maintain a positive financial position while meeting operational requirements.

-Supply Operations

Supply operations remained uninterrupted throughout the reporting period.

The Supply Corps continued sourcing ingredients and provisions required for the preparation of Elsweyr Fondue. Master Chef Thorandale has consistently maintained sourcing for required ingredients and production will begin very soon . 

- Equipment Status

Current armor inventories remain sufficient to support anticipated personnel intake.

Existing stock is adequate to equip incoming soldiers, while additional armor shipments from the East Empire Company (EEC) are expected to further strengthen reserve inventories. At present, no equipment shortages are anticipated. 

The Supply Corps remains in a stable operational and financial condition.
